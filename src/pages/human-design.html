What is Human Design?

Human Design is a tool for self-discovery. It uses your birth date, time, and place to create a personal chart ~ a map to explore how you use your energy, make choices, and move through life.

A reading helps you make sense of that map. Together, we turn the shapes and symbols into clear ideas you can try in your day-to-day life.

You do not need to fit yourself into a box. This is a way to get curious about what feels right for you, notice your patterns, and build trust in yourself.
