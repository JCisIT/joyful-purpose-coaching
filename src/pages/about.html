The choice that changed everything

I chose amazing.

I became a widow at the age of 20. Young. Devastated. Faced with a choice I never imagined I would have to make: throw my life away, or make it amazing.

I chose amazing.

Not because I felt amazing. Not because I had it all figured out. But because something deep inside me knew that this pain could become something more ~ if I was willing to transform it. That choice became the foundation of everything I teach today.

Music became my medicine. Songwriting became my therapy. And slowly, something shifted ~ my songs transformed from grief into power, and I discovered the tools that made it possible: NLP, mindset practices, tapping into my spiritual resources, and the science of subconscious change.

If these tools could transform my life after profound loss, they could transform anyone's life.
